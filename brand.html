Brand

August 31, 2026

Lucky number 13 for this piece!

August came and went and there were plenty of things to write about. Specifically, there were plenty of things to reflect on as I turned 27 in August and the world keeps on spinning. I thought about whether I wanted this piece to sway more personal or professional and settled on the latter. I like the freedom of being able to choose between personal or professional and the fearlessness I feel typing when the writing does lean more personal. I say all this as a coach of sorts had suggested it’s not worth writing unless you have something important to say and I disagreed. You can write and have it be meaningless or write and have it be the most profound thing in the world. There are no rules and encouraging constraints just makes people scared. I give my writing importance and it turns out I’ve got a lot to say.

Now, back to the good stuff! The “stuff” in this instance relates to the more “professional” aspects of my world.

There was a fascinating conversation I was privy to in the office about brands and winners and losers. The companies that get funded are the ones that people know about, brag about, and use even when the product of another is better. “Brand” in this aspect is different from “Marketing” where brand is the ethos, the “cool factor” the thing that makes people think of the company immediately. The brand is the thing infiltrating communities and power users and driving more and more customers to the platform. There is power with the brand when yielded correctly. The idea behind this emphasis is that the biggest buzz will invite the largest funding and the largest funding will ensure the best outcome. Even if there is no IPO in sight, the outcome will, at the minimum, return everyone’s capital.

Brand didn’t used to be this fascinating. Taking it back a few years, brand and marketing for a company were almost an after-thought. It was all about the product and the user-experience. Brand was a bonus or something confined to CPG companies only.

The idea of a brand surpassing product or tech in the race for the most important aspect of a business is shocking. Yet it’s so true! Speed to market has resurfaced as defensibility of companies becomes obsolete, and the brand of the business and the following it gains has now become more important than ever.

We’re observing very few winners in markets and these winners are becoming behemoths. In the AI agent world you have Decagon and Sierra as massive players and others competing such as Maven (disclosing this is a portco). Voice AI has ElevenLabs and prediction markets have Kalshi. There are many competitors, but only a few household names.

I think the valuations are inflated but I’m not convinced that even if these companies fail that they will fail dramatically. Their failure, if it comes, will be financially quiet and there will be life boats. The write-offs may bruise some egos but to avoid any other humiliation the best outcome will be analyzed over and over by the large firms that have the incentive, if only through optics, to make sure the company ends up okay.

So then the question is, how do you become one of the behemoths through a brand? Every piece of content feels more and more saturated every day and the prescriptive posting at the cost of creativity is unbearable to read. Creativity aside, the most practical starting point seems to be in identifying the buyer vs. the champion. We stress ICP so much but we forget to take into account the ones whispering in the ICP’s ears. A holistic evaluation of who to target and with what information to target them with is step one.

Our portfolio is actively working through this shift and no one has found the perfect answer just yet. There are no playbooks to follow but I do believe top-down leadership has a huge role to play in the success or failure of this aspect of the business.

Emphasis on brand has got me thinking about personal brand. Is personal brand always authentic to who we are or is it built for who we want to sell to, and is this a choice a person individually makes?

Brand, brand, brand, it’s all about brand now and I don’t think we’re, or maybe I’m, ready! As I added a year around the sun, I realized I don’t invest a lot into my personal brand. Maybe this year will change that and my own discoveries will help founders discover some of their own.

Brandless, but willing to work on it.
